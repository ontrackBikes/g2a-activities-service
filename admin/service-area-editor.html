Service Area Editor

Areas are broad regions (e.g. Andaman & Nicobar). Locations are specific zones/islands that belong to an Area.
Accepts a Polygon, a MultiPolygon, a Feature<Polygon|MultiPolygon>, or a FeatureCollection containing one such feature. Multi-part shapes can only be pasted here, not drawn.
Or use the draw tools on the map (top-left) to draw / edit / delete the polygon directly. Drawing a new shape replaces the current one.
